<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["New feature"]}>
## Projects

You can now organize work into **projects** — a new layer above channels that groups related boards, issues, and conversations together. Create a project from the sidebar, connect it to a channel, and manage all of its board cards in one place. A new **Issues** view in the sidebar lets you see every issue assigned to you across all projects at a glance.

</Update>

<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["New feature"]}>
## Billing and usage dashboard

Proddy now has built-in billing. From the new **Billing** page in your workspace settings, you can view your current plan, upgrade to Pro or Enterprise, and manage your subscription. A companion **Usage** dashboard shows how much of your plan's limits you've used for AI requests, messages, tasks, channels, boards, and notes — so you always know where you stand.

</Update>

<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["New feature"]}>
## Plan-based limits

Workspaces now enforce usage limits based on your pricing plan. Free workspaces include generous allowances for messages, tasks, AI requests, and more. When you approach a limit, you'll see a prompt to upgrade. Pro and Enterprise plans unlock higher (or unlimited) thresholds across every feature.

</Update>

<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## Boards revamp

[Boards](/Collaboration/Boards) got a major refresh. The issue drawer now includes an activity log, watchers, sub-tasks, comments, and richer detail editing. The Gantt view has been rebuilt with zoom controls and smoother navigation. You can also search and filter cards directly from the board header.

</Update>

<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## Improved chat editor and threads

The message composer has been redesigned with a cleaner layout and better formatting controls. Threads now support editable titles and an improved reply experience, making it easier to keep conversations organized inside channels.

</Update>

<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## AI-powered search

Search across your workspace now uses AI to surface more relevant results. You can find messages, tasks, and content faster using natural-language queries.

</Update>

<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## Web push notifications

Proddy now sends real-time push notifications in your browser so you never miss a message or update — even when the app isn't in the foreground.

</Update>

<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## Sidebar and navigation improvements

The workspace sidebar now includes a dedicated **Projects** section, an **Issues** shortcut, and quick links to **Billing** and **Usage**. The top bar has also been refined for a cleaner look across all workspace pages.

</Update>

<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["Bugfix"]}>
## Bug fixes and performance

- Fixed a layout issue in the chat input on smaller screens.
- Resolved a sidebar rendering glitch.
- Improved overall stability with various under-the-hood code quality fixes.

</Update>
