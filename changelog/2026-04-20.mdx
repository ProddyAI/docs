<Update label="April 20, 2026" tags={["New feature"]}>
## AI assistant

Meet **Proddy AI** — your personal work assistant, available from the dashboard. Ask it about your schedule, tasks, board cards, or anything in your workspace. Proddy pulls real-time data using built-in tools so you get answers grounded in your actual work, not guesses.

</Update>

<Update label="April 20, 2026" tags={["New feature"]}>
## Third-party integrations

Connect your favorite tools directly to Proddy. You can now link **GitHub**, **Gmail**, **Slack**, **Linear**, **Notion**, and **ClickUp** from the [Manage](/Account/Manage) page. Once connected, the AI assistant can read and act on data from those services on your behalf.

</Update>

<Update label="April 20, 2026" tags={["New feature"]}>
## Slack import

Migrating from Slack? You can now import channels, messages, and user data straight into your Proddy workspace. Head to the **Import Data** section in [Manage](/Account/Manage) to connect your Slack workspace and start a one-click import.

</Update>

<Update label="April 20, 2026" tags={["New feature"]}>
## Audio rooms

Channels and canvases now support live **audio rooms**. Jump into a voice call with your team without leaving Proddy — join, mute, and leave directly from the [Canvas](/Collaboration/Canvas) toolbar or channel header.

</Update>

<Update label="April 20, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## AI-powered flowchart generation

The [Canvas](/Collaboration/Canvas) now includes an AI flowchart generator. Describe what you need in plain language and Proddy creates a Mermaid diagram on your canvas automatically.

</Update>

<Update label="April 20, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## Daily recaps and weekly digests

Stay on top of what happened while you were away. **Daily recaps** give you an AI-generated summary of workspace activity for any day. **Weekly digest emails** arrive every Monday morning with key stats across all your workspaces — messages sent, tasks completed, and top active channels.

</Update>

<Update label="April 20, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## Note export

You can now export any note to **PDF**, **Markdown**, **HTML**, or **JSON** from the [Notes](/Collaboration/Notes) editor. Share your work outside Proddy or back it up in whatever format you prefer.

</Update>

<Update label="April 20, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## Email notifications

Proddy now sends email notifications for **mentions**, **direct messages**, **thread replies**, and **board card assignments**. You can manage your email preferences from your account settings, and every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.

</Update>

<Update label="April 20, 2026" tags={["Update"]}>
## Workspace reports

The [Reports](/Workspace/reports) page now includes richer analytics — an overview dashboard, channel activity breakdown, content analysis, performance metrics, and user activity views. All charts support time-range filtering (1 day, 7 days, or 30 days) and can be exported to PDF.

</Update>
